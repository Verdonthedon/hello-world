# 03 — Recreation: Script, Storyboard, Captions & Audio

**Recreation concept (same lane, fresh subject):**
> **"Humanoid home robots look amazing in the ads… but reality:"**

We keep @longliveai's winning **expectation-vs-reality / robot-curiosity** format
(file 02) but swap delivery robots → **humanoid household robots** (the hottest AI
topic of 2026), so it's a fresh post in their proven format, not a copy.

- **Title/working name:** `home-robot-promo-vs-reality`
- **Length:** 11 seconds · **Aspect:** 9:16 (1080×1920) · **FPS:** 30
- **Tone:** Slick → comedic turn. Faceless. Daily-AI brand voice.

---

## A. Hook variants (A/B test — first frame text)

1. **"Home robots look INCREDIBLE in the ads… 🤖"** *(primary)*
2. "They promised us robot butlers in 2026…"
3. "This is the home robot they sold you… vs the one you get."
4. "Everyone wants a humanoid robot. Then this happens 👇"

> Hook rule: the payoff is **withheld**; the word "but reality" never appears until the TURN.

---

## B. Shot-by-shot storyboard (11s)

| # | Time | Segment | Shot description | Camera/grade | On-screen text |
|---|------|---------|------------------|--------------|----------------|
| 1 | 0.0–1.2s | HOOK/SETUP | Sleek humanoid robot pours coffee in a sunlit modern kitchen, smiling family in soft focus | Smooth gimbal push-in, warm/bright, lens flare | **"Home robots look INCREDIBLE in the ads…"** (upper third, bold white) |
| 2 | 1.2–3.0s | SETUP | Robot folds laundry perfectly, hands a child a toy, glides across spotless floor | Cinematic, saturated, slow-mo | "…in the promo videos" |
| 3 | 3.0–3.8s | **TURN** | Hard cut to black 2 frames → CCTV/phone look. Text slams in | Jarring cut, audio flip | **"…but reality:"** (centered, big) |
| 4 | 3.8–5.4s | PAYOFF 1 | Same robot wobbles, knocks over a vase, freezes mid-step | Handheld/CCTV, desaturated, slight grain | caption pop: "it knocked over grandma's vase" |
| 5 | 5.4–7.0s | PAYOFF 2 | Robot tangled in the laundry it was folding; cat watching | Phone-shot vibe | "tangled in the laundry 💀" |
| 6 | 7.0–8.8s | PAYOFF 3 | Robot stuck in a doorway, gently bumping the frame on a loop | Static security-cam angle | "stuck. just… stuck." |
| 7 | 8.8–10.0s | BUTTON | Freeze on the toppled robot; quick zoom-punch | Freeze + 5% punch-in | "we're not ready 😅" |
| 8 | 10.0–11.0s | LOOP | Cut back to shot 1 (pristine kitchen) to loop the hook | Match the opening frame | "Follow for your daily AI 🤖" |

**Editing notes**
- TURN at ~32% (early) to maximize retention; the audio change is the dopamine beat.
- Grade contrast is the joke: **warm+saturated promo** vs **cool+degraded reality**.
- Captions: word-by-word pop, bold, keyword highlighted (e.g. yellow on "reality").
- Subtle 2–4% zoom drift on static shots so nothing feels frozen.
- Clean loop: last frame ≈ first frame.

## E. Audio plan

- **Bed A (0–3s):** bright, optimistic, "future is here" synth swell.
- **TURN sting (3.0–3.8s):** record-scratch / tape-stop / beat-drop.
- **Bed B (3.8–10s):** comedic, slightly awkward, light ambient.
- **Button (10–11s):** quick resolve note that loops back to Bed A.
- **Alt:** one trending audio whose built-in drop is aligned to the 3.0s TURN.
- Duck all music –12 dB under the voiceover; keep hook text readable in silence (many watch muted → captions carry it).
