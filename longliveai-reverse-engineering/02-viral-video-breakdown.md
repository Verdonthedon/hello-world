## Reel-genre: "Expectation vs. Reality" (robot edition)

This is one of the most reliable short-form formats. The structure:

```
HOOK (withhold) → SETUP (the glossy promise) → TURN (the "but reality") → PAYOFF (the funny/awkward truth) → LOOP
```

## Reconstructed beat sheet (~10–12s, 9:16)

| Time | Beat | Visual | On-screen text (hook/caption) | Audio |
|------|------|--------|-------------------------------|-------|
| 0.0–1.0s | **HOOK** | Sleek, cinematic promo shot of a delivery robot gliding through a sunny, perfect city street | **"Delivery robots look futuristic…"** (bold, upper third) | Bright, optimistic swell / trending sound starts |

## Why each beat earns the next

- **0–3s retention engineered by the hook**: "look futuristic… *but reality*" — the payoff is withheld, so the viewer must keep watching to resolve the gap.
- **The TURN at ~40%**: the hard cut + audio change is the dopamine beat; it's the most-shared moment and the reason people tag friends.
- **PAYOFF = relatable + funny**: schadenfreude at over-hyped tech is universally shareable, especially in the AI-skeptic comment crowd.
- **Clean loop**: ending on the pristine promo shot rejoins the opening, inflating replay count.

- Reality segment = handheld/CCTV/phone aesthetic, slightly degraded, comedic.
- The **contrast between the two grades** is the visual joke — preserve it.
- Bold upper-third hook text; word-by-word highlighted captions lower-third.
