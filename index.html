Questions

Before you apply.

Who is this retreat for?

Anyone ready to get real with themselves and reconnect with truth and purpose. If you're seeking clarity, breakthroughs, peace, or a deeper connection to life, this space is for you. All beliefs and all genders are welcome.

Where and when does it take place?

Manggis, Bali — a quiet, sacred pocket of the island surrounded by jungle, ocean, and mountains. The retreat runs March 3–7, 2026: five days and four nights.

How many people will be there?

The circle is intentionally capped at 12 participants. This keeps the container intimate, the coaching personal, and the connection real.

Can I come solo?

Absolutely. Many people arrive on their own — it's part of the magic. You come as an individual and leave with a family.

What's included in the investment?

Accommodation, a private chef and all meals, one sacred ceremony, one breathwork journey, daily meditation and coaching, sunrise walks, and sharing circles. Flights to Bali are not included.

How do I secure a spot?

Submit an application above. We'll reach out within 48 hours to schedule a short discovery call, share full pricing, and make sure it's the right fit — both ways.
